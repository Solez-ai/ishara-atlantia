ISHARA
On-Device Sign Language Recognition for Everyone
AI/ML • Computer Vision
The Problem

72 million Deaf and hard-of-hearing people worldwide lack digital sign language tools. Current solutions require expensive GPUs and cloud infrastructure, excluding developing nations.

Bangladesh: 500,000+ Deaf individuals need accessible technology

Our Solution
  • Lightweight: 150 KB models
  • On-Device: CPU-friendly, <10ms
  • Multilingual: BdSL, ASL, extensible
  • Real-Time: 20-30 FPS live recognition
  • Privacy: No cloud transmission
How It Works

4-Step Pipeline:

  1. Capture video frames (30 FPS)
  2. Detect hand/pose landmarks (MediaPipe)
  3. Extract 144D feature vectors
  4. Classify with lightweight ML (<1ms)
Technology Stack
  • MediaPipe (hand detection)
  • scikit-learn (classification)
  • Python 3.10+
  • OpenCV, NumPy
  • Session-held-out CV
